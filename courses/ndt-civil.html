CIVIL NDT

NDT – Civil

Non-destructive testing of concrete and structures, in theory and in the field.

10 days STED Council, Govt. of India · ASNT (American Society for Nondestructive Testing)
[ OVERVIEW ]

About this course

A detailed theoretical and practical course on applying NDT techniques to civil structures, so you can assess concrete quality and structural condition without damaging the structure.

[ CURRICULUM ]

What you will learn

  • 01Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) testing
  • 02Rebound hammer testing
  • 03Radiography of concrete
  • 04Rebar location and cover measurement
  • 05Interpreting and reporting results

Practical, hands-on training

Sessions are taught by faculty with long professional and teaching backgrounds, on real equipment and specimens, with classroom theory alongside.
